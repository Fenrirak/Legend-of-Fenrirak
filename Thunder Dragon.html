The Thunder Dragon

The full board game, online, for 2 to 6 players. Pick a Knight, an Archer or a Mage, race across the map, gather the three key fragments, and be the first to bring down Fenrirak.

How it plays

Roll one die to move. Land on a square and do what it says. Monsters are run by the computer — they roll to hit just like you do, and always use the hardest attack they have the energy for. In battle, an even roll is a hit and an odd roll is a miss; damage is your attack minus the target's defence. Beat Seraphina, the Goblin Scout Group and the Goblin Hideout to collect all three key fragments, open the Gate past the Fountain of Life, and enter the arena. Land on the same square as another player and you fight them — that part is not optional.
